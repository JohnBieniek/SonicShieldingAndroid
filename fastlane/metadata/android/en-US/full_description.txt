Sonic Shielding provides quick access to a locally stored audio comfort profile. Tap the app icon or Quick Settings tile to turn protection on and off. Long-press the app icon to open the nine-band Comfort Profile.

On Android devices that expose global output-mix audio effects to third-party apps, Sonic Shielding applies the selected frequency reductions across device playback. Support varies by manufacturer and Android version.

Sonic Shielding requests no microphone permission, records no audio, uses no analytics, and sends nothing to a server. It is a comfort tool—not a medical device or hearing test.
